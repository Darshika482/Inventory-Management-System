# Product video v2 — script (draft 3, for review)

Lagbhag 77 second, 9:16, Hinglish awaaz aur bade captions, WhatsApp par bhejne ke liye.

**Idea:** Dukaan 9 baje band hoti hai, par hisaab raat 12 baje tak chalta hai.

---

## Scene 1: Raat ki dukaan (0–6 sec)

Screen par: Raat. Shutter aadha neeche, ek bulb jal raha hai. Dukaandaar akela counter par, saamne mota register aur bills ka dher. Ghadi mein 11:40.

Awaaz: "Dukaan nau baje band ho gayi thi. Phir aap abhi tak yahan kyun baithe hain?"

Text: **11:40 PM. Dukaan band. Kaam chalu.**

---

## Pehli pareshani: party ka bill chadhana

### Scene 2: Pareshani (6–13 sec)

Screen par: Counter par mota, bada register khula hai, saath mein party ka lamba bill aur transport ki bilty. Dukaandaar pen se ek-ek item register mein utaar raha hai: naam, quantity, rate. Phir calculator: transport ka bhada jodo, GST jodo, "maal kitne ka pada" nikaalo. Ek jagah kaat-peet. Total bill se match nahi hua. Peeche laptop par bhi wahi kaam chal raha hai, ek ladka type kar raha hai.

Awaaz: "Maal aaya, saath mein chaalis item ka bill. Register mein utaaro ya laptop mein chadhao: kya aaya, kitna aaya, transport ka kitna gaya, maal kitne ka pada. Ek galti, aur poora hisaab galat."

Text: **Kya aaya? Kitne ka pada?** → **40 item. Ek-ek karke.** → **Hisaab galat.**

### Scene 3: Hal, jaadu wala moment (13–29 sec) — sabse bada scene

Screen par: Sab ruk jaata hai. Register band hota hai. Dukaandaar phone nikaalta hai, bill ke upar le jaata hai. Ek pal ki khamoshi. *Click.* Bill apne aap bharne lagta hai: party ka naam, bill number, date, transport ka naam aur bilty number. Phir items ek-ek karke girte hain, quantity, rate, amount ke saath, tez aur tez. Phir GST. Phir total, bilkul wahi jo kaagaz par likha hai. Bhara hua bill screen par ruka rehta hai.

Awaaz: "Ab na register, na laptop. Sirf ek photo. Party ka naam, bill number, har item, quantity, rate, GST, transport... sab apne aap bhar gaya. Ek baar dekhiye, aur save."

Text: **Sirf ek photo.** → **Bill apne aap bhar gaya.** → **Likhna: zero.**

---

## Doosri pareshani: counter par bill banana

### Scene 4: Pareshani (29–34 sec)

Screen par: Din ka time, counter par teen-chaar grahak khade hain. Dukaandaar bill book mein haath se likh raha hai, calculator par jod raha hai. Grahak ghadi dekh raha hai. Payment ke time grahak phone mein rakam khud type karta hai, aur galat daal deta hai.

Awaaz: "Counter par bheed hai. Bill haath se likho, total jodo. Aur grahak UPI mein rakam hi galat daal de."

Text: **Grahak khada hai. Bill ban raha hai.**

### Scene 5: Hal (34–46 sec)

Screen par: Bade buttons par kuch tap, bill tayyar. Raseed print hoti hai: upar dukaan ka naam, neeche dukaan ka apna QR. Grahak scan karta hai, rakam pehle se bhari hui hai, bas pay dabana hai. Phir wahi bill grahak ke WhatsApp par pahunchta hai.

Awaaz: "Ab kuch hi tap mein bill tayyar. Aapki dukaan ka naam, aur aapka apna QR. Grahak scan kare, rakam pehle se bhari hui. Paisa seedha aapke account mein, aur bill uske WhatsApp par."

Text: **Aapke naam ka bill. Aapka apna QR.** → **Scan. Pay. Ho gaya.**

---

## Teesri pareshani: udhaar

### Scene 6: Pareshani (46–52 sec)

Screen par: Register ke panne palat rahe hain, ek naam dhoondh rahe hain. Kahin kaat-peet, kahin alag se parchi lagi hai. Phir dukaandaar ek-ek grahak ko phone lagata hai: ek uthata nahi, doosra kehta hai "kal deta hoon".

Awaaz: "Kis par kitna udhaar hai? Register mein dhoondo. Phir ek-ek ko phone lagao. Koi uthata nahi, koi kehta hai, kal."

Text: **Kiska kitna baaki?** → **"Kal deta hoon."**

### Scene 7: Hal (52–65 sec)

Screen par: Udhaar ki list. Jis par sabse zyada baaki, woh sabse upar; poora total upar likha hai. "Select all" dabta hai. Ek-ek karke har grahak ke WhatsApp par ek saaf picture jaati hai: kitna baaki hai, bill-wise, aur pay karne ka QR. Phir payment aane lagte hain aur rakam ghat-te ghat-te zero.

Awaaz: "Ab poora udhaar ek nazar mein. Sabko chuniye, aur har grahak ke WhatsApp par reminder: poora hisaab, aur pay karne ka QR. Na phone, na message likhna. Grahak scan kare, aur udhaar wapas."

Text: **Poora udhaar, ek nazar mein.** → **Reminder WhatsApp par, QR ke saath.** → **Udhaar wapas.**

---

## Scene 8: Aur bhi hai (65–70 sec)

Screen par: Chaar card tezi se nikalte hain.

Awaaz: "Aur haan: stock ka hisaab, staff ki salary, Hindi mein bhi, aur bina internet ke bhi."

Text: **Stock** · **Staff salary** · **हिंदी** · **Bina internet**

---

## Scene 9: Ghar chalo (70–77 sec)

Screen par: Wahi dukaan. Ghadi mein 9:05. Register band, light band, shutter poora neeche. Dukaandaar sirf phone haath mein lekar ghar ja raha hai.

Awaaz: "Dukaan nau baje band. Aur hisaab bhi nau baje band. Poora business, aapke phone mein."

Text: **Dukaan 9 baje band. Hisaab bhi.** + app ka naam aur contact

---

## Jo abhi tay hona hai

1. Aakhri screen par app ka naam aur contact (number ya link).
2. Kya app transport ka bhada jod kar "maal kitne ka pada" khud nikaalta hai? Haan ho toh Scene 3 mein line judegi.
3. Dukaan kis tarah ki dikhani hai (kirana, hardware, kapda).
4. Awaaz male ya female.

## Daave kahan rukte hain

- Photo se supplier (party) ka bill bharta hai, aur app save se pehle lines check karne ko kehta hai. Isliye line hai "ek baar dekhiye, aur save".
- Reminder mein grahak ek baar chune jaate hain, phir har grahak ke liye WhatsApp khulta hai aur Send dabana hota hai. Script "ek tap mein sabko" nahi kehti.
- "3 ghante bachao" jaise number nahi hain, kyunki unke peeche data nahi hai.

# Product video v2 — script (draft 1, for review)

**Format:** 9:16, about 70 seconds, Hinglish voiceover with big on-screen captions (works on mute), made to be forwarded on WhatsApp.

**Idea in one line:** *Dukaan 9 baje band hoti hai, par hisaab 12 baje tak chalta hai.* The app gives the shop owner those hours back.

**What changed from the old video:** the old one was a 3-minute tour of every menu, in menu order. This one tells one story about one shopkeeper's evening, shows only four things, and spends the most time on the one that looks like magic: photo → bill.

## How each part is meant to land

- **Scenes 1–2 are the "yes, that is me" part.** The viewer should recognise their own evening before they see any app. No app screen appears in the first 13 seconds.
- **Scene 4 gets the most time and the biggest build-up.** Sound drops out before the click, items land on the beat, the total matching the paper is the payoff. Everything else in the video is shorter so this stands out.
- **Stock and salary get six seconds in total**, as "and it also does this".
- **The ending answers the opening**: same shop, same shutter, closed on time.

## Claims, and where they stop

The pain is dramatised; what the app does is shown as it really works.

- Photo → bill reads the **supplier's (party) bill**, the one you get when goods arrive. It fills party, bill number, date, items, discounts and GST. The app asks the owner to check the lines before saving, so the line is "ek baar dekhiye, aur save", not "100% sahi".
- Reminders: the owner picks customers once, then WhatsApp opens on each customer's chat with the picture attached and the owner taps Send for each one. The script says "sabko chuniye", not "ek tap mein sabko chala gaya".
- No numbers such as "3 ghante bachao" or "10,000 dukaan" are used, because there is no data behind them.

## To decide before the video is made

1. **Name and ending:** what should the last screen say? The app name, and a phone/WhatsApp number or a link.
2. **Does saving a photo-filled bill also add the goods to stock?** If yes, scene 4 can end on "aur stock bhi apne aap badh gaya", which makes the moment stronger. It is left out for now.
3. **Shop type shown:** a general/hardware/cloth shop? The bill items and the shopkeeper's look follow from this.
4. **Voice:** male or female, and how energetic (calm and confident, or loud ad-style).
5. **Length:** 70 seconds as written, or a tighter 45-second cut (drops scene 7 and shortens 5 and 6).
